whether the symlink survives extraction:
Yes. The unzipped tarball is extracted using `tar -xvzf "$extractDir/deploy-artifact.tar.gz" -C "$firebasePublicDir"`, which preserves the attacker-controlled symlink inside the extracted directory (e.g. `leak -> /etc`).

whether deploy succeeds:
Yes. The `action-hosting-deploy` wraps `firebase-tools`, and `firebase deploy --only hosting` does not crash on symlinks.

whether the deployed preview serves out-of-tree content:
Yes. The Firebase CLI uses `glob` with `follow: true` to discover files to upload (`firebase-tools/lib/listFiles.js`). It actively traverses the symlink, reads the out-of-tree files (like `/etc/passwd`), computes their hashes, and uploads the privileged runner's filesystem to Firebase Hosting. The out-of-tree content is served publicly at the preview URL.
